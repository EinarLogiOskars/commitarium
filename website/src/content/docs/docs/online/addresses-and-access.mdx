This page shows you how to give a deployed app its address, and choose who can open it.

## Give it an address

On the **Deployment** page, under **Give it an address so people can open it**, choose a name, such as `todo`. The app then opens at `todo.example.com`, under [your domain](/docs/online/your-domain/).

Names use lowercase letters, digits and hyphens. A few names are reserved, such as `sign-in`.

## Who can open it

Under **Who can open it**, choose one:

- **Anyone, no password.** The app is public.
- **Only with a password.** Visitors sign in with the address name as the username (`todo`), and a password you choose. You can show the password again later, to share it, or set a new one.
- **Company sign-in.** People sign in with their work account, and you can choose which groups may open the app. See [Company sign-in](/docs/company-sign-in/overview/).

<Screenshot id="online-access" alt="Who can open it: Anyone, Only with a password, or Company sign-in, with the password field shown" />

How strong the password is, is up to you.

## Changing the address

You can rename the address at any time. Before you do, Commitarium warns you: the old address **stops working** once you change it, and anyone with the old link needs the new one.

Removing the address takes the app off the internet, but the deployment keeps running, ready for a new address.
