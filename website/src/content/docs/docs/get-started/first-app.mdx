This page walks you through making an app from one sentence, and changing it.

Before you start, [connect at least one agent](/docs/get-started/agents/) and choose Mayka's agent.

## 1. Describe it

1. In **Apps**, choose **New app**. Mayka's chat opens.
2. Say what you want, in your own words. For example: "I need an app to track when my plants need water."

If the request is clear, Mayka answers with a card: **New app**, its name, and a short summary of what she will build, with the things she had to guess. Choose **Create app**.

If it needs more, she asks a few short questions first, and looks up facts you shouldn't have to supply, such as tax rules or file formats. You can say "go for it" at any point, and she builds from what she has, filling the gaps with sensible defaults.

<Screenshot id="new-app" alt="Mayka's chat with a New app card and its Create app button" />

:::tip
Say who the app is for and what matters most. "A simple tracker for my own plants, mostly on my phone" gets a better first version than "a plant app".
:::

## 2. While it builds

Mayka now:

1. writes the app's **vision**: what it is for, and a plan split into priorities;
2. picks a **stack**, the tools the app is built with, and sets it up;
3. starts **autopilot**, which builds the plan piece by piece, each piece planned, built and reviewed by your agents.

The app's tile in Apps shows where it is: **Planning**, **Setting up**, then building. Open it to watch.

The app's window shows a **checklist** of the plan, with a tick for each piece that is done. The first piece is the base the rest builds on, and usually the longest step.

<Screenshot id="app-building" alt="An app's window while it builds, with the checklist of its plan" />

A simple app takes about half an hour. You can close the window, or do something else: the work carries on.

## 3. P0 means usable

The plan is ordered by priority. **P0** holds what the app needs to be useful at all; **P1** what comes next, and so on.

In the checklist, a line under the P0 pieces says **Usable from here**. Once those are done, the window fades from the checklist to your app. The later pieces keep arriving after that, in a small card in a corner, and the app may restart now and then while they do.

## 4. Open it

When the tile says **Ready**, choose it to open the app in its own window. Use it as you would any app. Its data stays between restarts.

If an app needs your help, its tile says **Needs you**, and the window says what to do in plain words.

## 5. Ask for a change

In the app's window, the round button in the corner opens Mayka's chat for this app. Ask for what you want, in plain words: "Add a thirst bar to each plant."

Mayka turns it into work for your agents and builds it at once. When it is done, the app reloads with the change, and your data stays. A small change takes a few minutes.

More in [Asking for changes](/docs/apps/asking-for-changes/).
