## In short

**Commitarium is an AI coding harness that works like a disciplined team.** Every change is planned, built, reviewed by a second model, tested, and merged through a pull request, using your Claude or Codex subscription, or an API key.

**It opens as a desktop app with nothing to configure.** Install it, sign in your agents, and describe an app or bring your own repository.

**On your own server, it ships your work too.** Put Commitarium on a VPS or a homelab and it keeps working while your laptop is closed, and deploys your apps at their own addresses, with HTTPS, behind a password or your company's sign-in, backed up every night.

## Two ways to use it

### Apps, for anyone

Describe any app. Commitarium builds it, runs it, and changes it whenever you want.

You tell Commitarium what you need, in plain words. Its assistant, **Mayka**, plans the app, hands the work to coding agents, checks what they make, and opens the finished app on your computer. Want it different? Ask, and it changes. No code needed. Start with [Your first app](/docs/get-started/first-app/).

### A workshop, for developers

Behind the apps is a full software workshop, and you can point it at your own code. Turn on **Developer mode**, bring in a repository, and work with Mayka the way you would with a small team:

- **Planned work orders.** Mayka turns what you want into work orders, each planned, built, reviewed and merged on its own. Several run side by side. For something bigger, [plan mode](/docs/developer-mode/plan-mode/) writes a plan in commit-sized slices first.
- **Independent review.** A lead agent builds; a reviewer, ideally from another provider (Claude and Codex), challenges the plan and the code. No model signs off on its own work.
- **Real pull requests.** Every change is a pull request in a private Git server (Forgejo) on your machine, with the whole discussion kept as an audit trail. Nothing reaches your own repository until you [sync it](/docs/your-own-code/syncing/).
- **As much control as you want.** Approve each plan and merge yourself, or let Mayka [build everything](/docs/apps/assists-or-builds/) and only ask when a decision needs you. Choose the [models and efforts](/docs/get-started/agents/) per phase.
- **Your own server, if you like.** Agents keep working while your laptop is closed, and a server can [link to GitHub](/docs/your-own-code/syncing/) and [put apps online](/docs/online/your-domain/).

Start with [Your first project](/docs/get-started/first-project/).

## What makes it different

- **Good practice, enforced.** The plan comes before the code, a second model reviews both, checks must pass, and the work is brought up to date with main before it merges. Commitarium runs these steps; they don't depend on an agent choosing to follow them. It doesn't make every model's code good, and the extra care costs some time and usage, but nothing lands unreviewed or untested.
- **Uses the AI you already have.** Sign in with your Claude or ChatGPT (Codex) subscription, or add an API key; either works. With a subscription, Commitarium keeps an eye on its usage limits and spreads the work to fit; with a key, you pay your provider per use.
- **Yours, on your machines.** Your code, your apps and their data stay on your computer, or on a server of your own.
- **Recovers on its own.** Interrupted work retries, and a failure pauses a work order instead of ending it. When Mayka can't fix something, she tells you in plain words.

## Words you will see

- **Mayka** is your assistant. You talk to her; she talks to the agents.
- An **agent** is one of your AI accounts that does the coding: a Claude or Codex subscription, or an API key.
- An **app** is something Commitarium built for you. It opens in its own window.
- A **work order** is one piece of work: one change or one feature, planned, built, reviewed and merged.
- **Developer mode** shows the workshop: projects, chats, work orders and the code's history. Turn it on when you want to see what's going on, steer the work more closely, or work on your own code. You don't need it to have an app built for you.

## Where it runs

Everything runs in [Docker](https://www.docker.com/) on your computer: the assistant, the agents, a private Git server that keeps the history, and your apps. You can also [run it on a server](/docs/servers/why-a-server/) of your own, and from there [put apps online](/docs/online/your-domain/), even [behind your company's sign-in](/docs/company-sign-in/overview/).

Commitarium is local-first, but not offline. When an agent works, the AI provider you signed in with receives your prompts and the code it needs, under your account and that provider's terms. See [Security and your data](/docs/security/).

## What you need

- A computer with macOS, Windows or Linux, and Docker.
- A Claude or Codex subscription, or an API key for either. Both providers is best: one builds, the other reviews.
