## In one sentence

Describe any app. Commitarium builds it, runs it, and changes it whenever you want.

You tell Commitarium what you need, in plain words. Its assistant, **Mayka**, plans the app, hands the work to coding agents, checks what they make, and opens the finished app on your computer. Want it different? Ask, and it changes.

<Screenshot id="apps" alt="The Apps area in Commitarium, with app tiles and the New app button" />

## What makes it different

- **No coding needed.** You talk to one assistant. She takes care of the plan, the code, the setup, and the fixes when something breaks.
- **Yours, on your machines.** Your apps and their data stay on your computer, or on a server of your own that keeps working while your computer is off.
- **Uses the AI you already have.** You sign in with your Claude or Codex account. Commitarium keeps an eye on your usage limits and spreads the work to fit.
- **Checked, not just generated.** A second agent, ideally from another AI provider, reviews every plan and every change before it lands.

## Words you will see

- **Mayka** is your assistant. You talk to her; she talks to the agents.
- An **agent** is one of your AI accounts (Claude or Codex) that does the coding.
- An **app** is something Commitarium built for you. It opens in its own window.
- A **work order** is one piece of work: one change or one feature, planned, built, reviewed and merged.
- **Developer mode** shows the workshop behind your apps: projects, chats, work orders and the code's history. You don't need it to make apps.

## Where it runs

Everything runs in [Docker](https://www.docker.com/) on your computer: the assistant, the agents, a private Git server that keeps the history, and your apps. You can also [run it on a server](/docs/servers/why-a-server/) of your own, and from there [put apps online](/docs/online/your-domain/), even [behind your company's sign-in](/docs/company-sign-in/overview/).

Commitarium is local-first, but not offline. When an agent works, the AI provider you signed in with receives your prompts and the code it needs, under your account and that provider's terms. See [Security and your data](/docs/security/).

## What you need

- A computer with macOS, Windows or Linux, and Docker.
- A Claude or Codex account. Both is best: one builds, the other reviews.
