This page shows you how to connect the AI that does the work: a subscription you already pay for, or an API key.

An **agent** is one of your AI accounts, named as you like: a Claude or ChatGPT (Codex) subscription, or an API key. Mayka, your assistant, and all the work she starts run on your agents.

## Add an agent

1. Open **Agents** at the top of the window.
2. Give the agent a name, such as "Claude Max".
3. Choose its provider: **Claude** or **Codex**.
4. Choose **Add agent**, then connect it:
   - **Sign in** with your account in the browser. Claude gives you a code to paste back into Commitarium; Codex shows a code to confirm in the browser.
   - Or choose **Use an API key** and paste it.
5. When it shows **Connected**, it is ready.

An agent signs in once and can then lead or review any work.

## Why two providers

Work in Commitarium has a **lead**, who plans and builds, and a **reviewer**, who checks the plan and the code. They run as separate sessions, so no model signs off its own work. When the reviewer comes from another provider, it sees things with fresh eyes.

One agent works. Two, from different providers, work best.

## Usage limits

Every subscription has usage limits. Each subscription agent on the Agents page shows how much of each limit is used, as bars. An agent on an API key has no such windows: you pay your provider for what it uses, and it shows no bars.

- Mayka spreads new work over your agents, preferring the one with the most usage left.
- When an agent reaches a limit, its work pauses and says when the limit resets. Commitarium tries again every 15 minutes, and the work continues by itself once the limit resets.

## Mayka's agent and model

Mayka needs an agent of her own. Open **Agents > Mayka** and choose the **Agent** she runs on, and its model. It is used for every project and applies from her next turn.

Mayka shares that agent's usage limit with any work on it. She writes the plan of every app and every work order, so a strong model pays off here.

<Screenshot id="agents-mayka" alt="The Mayka tab on the Agents page, with her agent and model chosen" />

Mayka chooses the agents, models and effort of the work she starts herself. The **Models** tab shows the model tiers she picks from. She asks you before she uses the most expensive models.

## Signing in again

If an agent's sign-in expires, it shows **Needs sign-in** and the work that needs it waits. Choose **Sign in again**, and the work picks up where it stopped.
