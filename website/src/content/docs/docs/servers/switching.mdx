This page shows you how to move between this computer and your servers.

## The switcher

The menu under Commitarium's name shows where you are: **This computer**, or one of your servers. Choose another to switch. Only the window reloads.

<Screenshot id="place-switcher" alt="The menu under Commitarium's name, listing This computer and a server" />

**Connect a server…** in the same menu adds another one. See [Installing on a server](/docs/servers/installing/).

## Each keeps working

Switching doesn't stop anything. A server's agents carry on while you look at this computer, and this computer's do too, until you quit the app.

Each place has its own projects, apps, agents and settings. Nothing is shared between them.

## Naming and forgetting a server

**Settings > Server** lists your servers. There you can give one a name, or forget it on this computer.

## What is different on a server

A few things work differently on a server, because it can't see your computer's folders:

- [Importing](/docs/your-own-code/importing/) asks for a repository address, not a folder.
- [Syncing](/docs/your-own-code/syncing/) goes through a linked repository.
- [Putting apps online](/docs/online/your-domain/) and [company sign-in](/docs/company-sign-in/overview/) are only on a server.
