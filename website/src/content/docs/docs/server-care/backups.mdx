This page explains your server's backups, and how to get your work back from one.

## Nightly, by itself

A server backs itself up **each night**, at a time when no agent is working, and keeps the **last seven**. They are kept in `backups/` in the server's data directory (`/var/lib/commitarium`).

**Settings > Backup** lists them. **Back up now** makes one at once. While the server is busy backing up, the app says so; a backup waits until no work order is running.

## What is in a backup

- your projects and their code, with the full history;
- your chats with Mayka, and the agents' history;
- the data of your deployed apps, what visitors saved;
- authentik's people, groups and settings, if [you set it up](/docs/company-sign-in/authentik/).

## What is not

- **Agent sign-ins.** After restoring onto a new server, sign your agents in again.
- **Device keys.** Pair your devices again.
- **`node_modules` folders.** Agents install those again when they need them, and leaving them out keeps backups much smaller.

## Restore

Choose a backup in **Settings > Backup** and **Restore**. It replaces what is on the server with the backup. The backup is read in full and checked before anything is replaced. Projects, apps and authentik's people all come back, and deployed apps start again by themselves.

## Keep a copy elsewhere

Backups stay on the server, so they don't survive losing the server. A [linked repository](/docs/your-own-code/syncing/#on-a-server-a-linked-repository) keeps a copy of each project's code somewhere else.

:::caution
Backups are not encrypted. They hold your code, its history and your chats. Protect them as you would the projects themselves.
:::

## On this computer

On your own computer, **Settings > Backup** works by hand: **Create a backup…** saves one where you choose, and **Restore from a backup…** replaces this installation with one. Commitarium stops its services while it copies them, then starts again. Agent sign-ins are not included here either.
