This page shows you how to set up company sign-in by talking it through with Mayka.

If you are not sure where to start, let Mayka guide you.

## Start

1. On a deployed app's **Deployment** page, under **Who can open it**, choose **Company sign-in (set up first)**.
2. Choose **Set it up with Mayka**.

The project's chat opens, focused on company sign-in, and Mayka already knows your server's domain.

<Screenshot id="sign-in-mayka" alt="Mayka's chat focused on company sign-in, asking whether the company has its own provider" />

## Her questions

**Does your company have its own sign-in provider, or does it want one?**

- **It has one** (Microsoft 365 or Entra ID, Google Workspace, Okta, authentik, Keycloak, or another): she gives you the steps for that provider in a short list: the application to create, the sign-in address to register, where to find the issuer, client ID and secret, and how to put groups in the token. You then enter them in Settings. See [Connecting your provider](/docs/company-sign-in/connecting-your-provider/).
- **It wants one**: before she recommends anything, she asks **how many people** will sign in, and whether someone is comfortable running a small service with Docker.

## What she recommends

- **A small team**, a handful of people up to a few dozen: **Pocket ID**. It is light and simple; people sign in with passkeys. You run it yourselves, and she walks you through it: running it with Docker, giving it an https address, creating the first admin with a passkey, adding people and groups, and connecting it. She also tells you Commitarium can set up authentik instead, at more memory.
- **A bigger company**, or anyone who would rather not set anything up: **authentik**, which [Commitarium sets up on your server](/docs/company-sign-in/authentik/). She says what it costs first: 2 to 3 GB of memory, so a server of 8 GB or more.
- **Something else**: tell her what, and she helps with that.

## Your secrets stay out of the chat

Mayka never asks for the client secret. It goes in **Settings > Server > Company sign-in** only. If you paste one into the chat, she tells you to enter it in Settings and make a new one, since the chat keeps it.

## After it's set up

Ask Mayka to use who signed in: "Greet people by name", or "Show the admin page only to the managers group." She builds it on the [identity headers](/docs/company-sign-in/overview/#the-app-knows-who-signed-in).
