This page explains the two modes a project can be in, and how to choose.

Every project, and so every app, has one of two modes. They decide how much Mayka does without asking.

## Builds everything

Mayka owns the project. She writes the vision, sets up the stack, and creates and starts the work until it runs, including work orders you add yourself. She still asks before she uses the most expensive models.

Apps made with **New app** start in this mode. It is the right one if you just want the app.

## Assists me

You do the work, and Mayka helps. She suggests work as cards in the chat, and acts only when you approve a card or hand her something. Your own work orders stay yours. Autopilot that you start still runs on its own.

This suits developers who want to stay in charge, and projects you [imported](/docs/your-own-code/importing/).

## Switching

In [Developer mode](/docs/developer-mode/turning-it-on/), open **Settings** on the project's **Project** card and choose under **How Mayka works**. The change applies at once.

An imported project in Assists me also gets a **Build for me** card after [Mayka's first look](/docs/your-own-code/first-look/), which switches it in one click.

:::tip
Try Assists me for a while on an app to see each step: a merge then waits for you to approve it. Switch back when you have seen enough.
:::
