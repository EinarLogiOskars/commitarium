This page shows you how to change an app you already have.

:::note
A short video for this section is coming.
:::

## Ask in the app

1. Open the app from **Apps**.
2. Choose the round button in the corner of the app's window. Mayka's chat opens, scoped to this app.
3. Say what should change: "Make the buttons blue", "Add a notes field to each plant", "Sort the list by date".

In an app, Mayka drives. Your request becomes a piece of work that starts at once, with no cards to approve. She tells you what she is doing ("Making the buttons blue…") and when it is done.

When the change is merged, the app's window reloads with it. Your app's data is kept.

## When Mayka needs you

Mayka only interrupts you when she has to:

- a question about what you want that only you can answer;
- an expensive model she would like to use, which waits for your approval;
- a problem she cannot fix herself. She says what happened in plain words, with one thing to do.

The app's tile in **Apps** then says **Needs you**.

## Seeing the work

Each app is also a project. **Show the work** on an app opens its project, where you can follow every piece of work. You need [Developer mode](/docs/developer-mode/turning-it-on/) for that, but never for changing an app.

## Removing an app

You can remove an app from its tile in **Apps**. If work is still running on it, Commitarium says so, and **Stop and remove** stops that work first.
