This page shows you how to plan something bigger than a tweak before anything is built.

For a small change, just ask. For something bigger, like "let me share plants with my partner, so we both see what needs water", plan it first. In plan mode, nothing is built until you say so.

## Turn it on

Any of these turns plan mode on in the chat you are in:

- the switch on the **Plan** card;
- typing `/plan` in the chat;
- typing `/plan` followed by what you want, which turns it on and sends the message.

`/plan` again, or the switch, turns it off. You can keep the plan for next time or discard it. Nothing is built either way.

## Talk it through

Mayka asks what she needs to know: what it is for, the features, the data, the look, the details, and what matters most. She researches facts you shouldn't have to supply. As you talk, she keeps the plan up to date.

**Insights** on the Plan card shows what she works from: what the plan covers so far, the decisions made, the open questions, her research, and any documents.

## The written plan

When she has enough, Mayka writes the plan. You can also ask for it with **Write the plan**. **Open the plan** shows it: the goal, the decisions, and the work split into **slices**, each about the size of one commit-sized piece of work.

Read it. If something is wrong, say so in the chat, and she revises it.

## Build it

**Build it** locks the plan and hands its slices to autopilot, one work order each. Slices that don't depend on each other run side by side; the rest wait for what they build on. You can follow them on the **Work orders** card.

:::tip
A plan is kept in one chat at a time. If you turn plan mode on in another chat, you can move the planning there; the plan stays as it is.
:::
